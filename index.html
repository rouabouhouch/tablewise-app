A GOOD PLACE TO START

Your spreadsheet,
a little more understood.

Open a CSV or TSV with a header row. Tablewise finds numeric, categorical and ISO date columns, then calculates summaries and charts.

  1. Explore. Compare columns and look for patterns.
  2. Check. Review gaps, duplicate rows and unusual values.
  3. Predict. Choose an outcome and inputs. Train a small model and compare it with a simple baseline on held-out data.

Up to 10 MB, 20,000 rows and 80 columns. Comma, semicolon and tab separators are detected automatically. Numbers use decimal points, with no currency symbols or thousands separators. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD.

Empty cells, NA, N/A, null and NaN are treated as missing. Original values are retained for the table and export. Analysis and models stay in memory; refreshing clears uploaded data. No third-party scripts or analytics are loaded.

Predictions are exploratory. Correlation does not establish cause. The sample is synthetic, not real customer data.
