How Tablewise works

Open a CSV or TSV with a header row. Tablewise detects numeric, categorical and ISO date columns.

  1. Explore. Compare columns and look for patterns.
  2. Check. Review gaps, duplicate rows and unusual values.
  3. Predict. Choose an outcome and inputs, train a model, and compare it with a baseline on held-out rows.

Up to 10 MB, 20,000 rows and 80 columns. Comma, semicolon and tab separators are detected automatically. Use decimal points for numbers and YYYY-MM-DD for dates.

Empty cells, NA, N/A, null and NaN count as missing. Analysis and models stay in memory. Refreshing clears uploaded data. Export a report before leaving to keep your results.

Predictions are exploratory. Correlation does not establish cause. The sample contains synthetic bike trips.
